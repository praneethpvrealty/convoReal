Brand Guidelines · v1

The mark is a house
and a conversation.

ConvoReal turns WhatsApp threads into closed property deals. The identity says exactly that in one shape — a gable roof and a chat bubble sharing a single silhouette, with the reply picked out in gold.

01 · The mark

One silhouette, two readings

The outline is a pitched roof; the notch at the lower left is a message tail. Inside sit two bars — a left-aligned enquiry and a right-aligned reply. The reply is Signal Gold, because in this business the answer is the product.

02 · Lockups

Three arrangements, no others

The wordmark sets Convo in ink and Real in violet — the join is the whole idea, so never space, hyphenate or case it differently. ConvoReal is always one word with two capitals.

Horizontal — default. Site header, invoices, letterhead
Horizontal on dark — product UI, decks, video end cards
Stacked — square spaces, backdrops, print
Clear space — keep the mark's full height free on every side

Minimum sizes

Horizontal lockup: 112px wide on screen, 24mm in print. Mark alone: 20px. Below 32px use convoreal-favicon.svg, which thickens the bars so both survive the pixel grid.

03 · Colour

Violet carries the brand, gold marks the win

Violet is the constant. Gold appears only where something has been matched, caught or closed — a reply, a confirmed slot, a deal. Used sparingly it becomes a signal; used decoratively it becomes noise.

Two colours that are not brand colours

Channel Green #1FA855 marks WhatsApp-specific affordances only — connection status, session windows, delivery ticks. It never appears in the logo, headlines or marketing surfaces, because ConvoReal is not WhatsApp. Semantic red, amber and green for validation are functional UI colours and sit outside this palette.

The product ships six accent themes (violet, emerald, cobalt, amber, rose, verdant). Those recolour a customer's workspace; they never recolour the logo. Violet stays fixed wherever ConvoReal speaks as itself.

04 · Typography

Manrope speaks, Inter works

Manrope carries everything the brand says out loud — the wordmark, headlines, decks, video captions. Its flat-sided curves and tight joints hold up at display sizes without turning generic. Inter stays in the product UI, where it already ships and where neutrality is the point.

05 · App icons

Ship the square, let the platform mask it

The icon is a full-bleed violet tile with the mark knocked out in white. Export the square artwork at full size — iOS applies the squircle, Android the adaptive mask, WhatsApp and Google a circle. Rounding the corners yourself produces a visible double-cut.

07 · Misuse

The short list of nos

  • Don't add a drop shadow, outline, bevel or glow to the mark. The gradient is the only depth it gets.
  • Don't redraw the bars, swap their order, or make both gold — the enquiry is white, the reply is gold, and that asymmetry is the meaning.
  • Don't lock the mark up with another company's logo. Set partner marks beside it with clear space between, both at equal cap height.
  • Don't use the mark as a bullet, a loading spinner, or a repeating pattern tile.
